# Firewall & Security Refresh

## Context

Growth in users, devices, internet demand, cloud services, remote access, and endpoint integration had moved the existing perimeter platform beyond its ideal operational window.

## Challenge

Migrate to a higher-capacity security platform while maintaining service continuity and creating room for future network and security requirements.

## Constraints

- The perimeter platform was a critical dependency for the organisation
- Outage windows were limited
- Existing rules and integrations needed careful interpretation
- Security improvement had to be balanced with usability
- The new platform had to support growth beyond current demand

## My role

- Evaluated capacity, integration, operational, and lifecycle requirements
- Coordinated technical planning and supplier engagement
- Reviewed existing services and migration dependencies
- Planned cutover, testing, rollback, and post-migration validation
- Continued operational management of firewall, VPN, endpoint, and monitoring capabilities

## Approach

1. Treated the refresh as a service-continuity project, not a hardware replacement.
2. Reviewed required traffic flows, remote access, identity, endpoint, and monitoring dependencies.
3. Prepared a controlled migration plan with validation and rollback steps.
4. Confirmed business-critical services after cutover.
5. Used the refresh to improve capacity, supportability, and future integration.

## Outcome

The migration improved security-platform capacity and provided a more sustainable foundation for network growth, endpoint integration, remote connectivity, and ongoing policy management.

## Capabilities demonstrated

Firewall architecture · Security migration · VPN · Endpoint integration · Policy management · Capacity planning · Change control · Risk management · Vendor coordination · Incident troubleshooting
