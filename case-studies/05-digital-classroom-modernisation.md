# Digital Classroom Modernisation Programme

## Context

Teaching spaces required reliable combinations of endpoints, projection, interactive technology, sound, network connectivity, and user support. Individual equipment purchases were not enough; the environment needed a repeatable programme.

## Challenge

Modernise classroom technology while balancing teacher usability, equipment compatibility, support capacity, budgets, room differences, and long replacement lifecycles.

## My role

- Evaluated requirements with technical and user needs in mind
- Planned endpoint, display, audio, interactive, and connectivity improvements
- Coordinated suppliers, installation, testing, support, and replacement planning
- Standardised practical solutions where possible
- Resolved integration issues across infrastructure, audiovisual, and end-user systems

## Approach

1. Treated the classroom as an integrated service rather than a list of devices.
2. Considered the complete user journey from login and connectivity to presentation and sound.
3. Selected solutions that could be supported consistently by a small technical team.
4. Phased upgrades according to educational value, equipment condition, risk, and budget.
5. Included lifecycle planning so modernisation remained continuous rather than becoming a once-off project.

## Outcome

The programme created a more consistent foundation for technology-enabled teaching and reduced fragmentation by connecting user experience, infrastructure, support, and lifecycle decisions.
