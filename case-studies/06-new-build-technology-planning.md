# New-Build Technology Planning

## Context

Technology decisions made late in a building project are expensive to correct. New construction and refurbishment required early input covering connectivity, equipment locations, power, audiovisual services, security, and future expansion.

## Challenge

Translate operational technology requirements into practical building inputs before walls, ceilings, routes, and finishes limited the available options.

## My role

- Gathered current and future technology requirements
- Advised on structured cabling, fibre routes, equipment locations, and network coverage
- Considered audiovisual, security, power resilience, access, cooling, and support requirements
- Coordinated with management, contractors, suppliers, and other stakeholders
- Reviewed proposals and identified omissions before implementation

## Approach

1. Started with how spaces would be used rather than beginning with a device list.
2. Mapped services, pathways, equipment locations, power, coverage, access, and maintenance needs.
3. Allowed capacity for growth and technology change.
4. Identified dependencies between networking, electrical work, ceilings, furniture, audiovisual systems, and physical security.
5. Documented requirements early enough to influence design and procurement.
6. Reviewed implementation against the intended operational outcome.

## Outcome

Early technology involvement reduced avoidable rework and produced spaces better prepared for connectivity, teaching technology, security, support, and future expansion.
