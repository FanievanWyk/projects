# Infrastructure Assessment Checklist

A vendor-neutral starting point for understanding an unfamiliar environment.

## Business and service context

- [ ] Identify critical services and owners
- [ ] Understand operating hours and maintenance constraints
- [ ] Record known pain points and recurring incidents
- [ ] Clarify growth, compliance, budget, and continuity expectations

## Inventory and lifecycle

- [ ] Hardware, software, licences, warranties, and support status
- [ ] Server, endpoint, network, storage, and security estate
- [ ] Cloud services and third-party dependencies
- [ ] Capacity, age, replacement priority, and technical debt

## Identity and access

- [ ] Identity sources and trust relationships
- [ ] Privileged access and role separation
- [ ] Joiner, mover, and leaver processes
- [ ] Multifactor authentication and recovery controls
- [ ] Service accounts and credential ownership

## Network and connectivity

- [ ] Physical and logical topology
- [ ] Internet, WAN, routing, switching, and wireless
- [ ] Segmentation and policy boundaries
- [ ] Resilience, monitoring, capacity, and coverage
- [ ] Remote access and third-party connectivity

## Compute, storage, and platforms

- [ ] Physical and virtual workloads
- [ ] Operating-system and application support status
- [ ] Storage architecture and growth
- [ ] Clustering, redundancy, and failure domains
- [ ] Monitoring, patching, and administration

## Security and continuity

- [ ] Firewall, endpoint, email, and cloud security
- [ ] Vulnerability and patch-management practices
- [ ] Logging, alerting, incident response, and escalation
- [ ] Backup scope, separation, retention, and restore evidence
- [ ] Disaster-recovery dependencies and decision owners

## Operations

- [ ] Documentation quality and currency
- [ ] Service desk, escalation, and recurring workload
- [ ] Supplier, contract, and licence ownership
- [ ] Change, incident, problem, and asset practices
- [ ] Skills concentration and single-person dependencies

## Output

Produce a prioritised roadmap separating:

1. Immediate risk reduction
2. Stability and supportability
3. Capacity and lifecycle
4. Security and continuity
5. Strategic improvement and automation
