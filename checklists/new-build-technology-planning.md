# New-Build Technology Planning Checklist

Use this checklist early in construction or refurbishment planning.

## Space and use

- [ ] Purpose, occupancy, flexibility, and expected equipment
- [ ] User journey, accessibility, and support model
- [ ] Furniture, teaching, meeting, presentation, or operational layouts

## Pathways and connectivity

- [ ] Incoming services and diverse routes
- [ ] Fibre backbone and structured cabling
- [ ] Containment, ceiling access, sleeves, and spare capacity
- [ ] Equipment-room locations, access, cooling, and security
- [ ] Wired outlets and wireless coverage expectations

## Power and resilience

- [ ] Equipment loads and dedicated circuits
- [ ] UPS, generator, surge, and shutdown requirements
- [ ] Power and data coordination at user and equipment locations
- [ ] Monitoring and maintenance access

## Audiovisual and collaboration

- [ ] Display, projection, audio, microphones, and control
- [ ] Video conferencing, streaming, and recording
- [ ] Cable routes
- [ ] Standard interfaces and supportability

## Security and life-safety integration

- [ ] Access control and hardware coordination
- [ ] CCTV coverage objectives and lawful placement
- [ ] Alarm, intercom, visitor, and emergency requirements
- [ ] Network, power, storage, and management dependencies

## Delivery and handover

- [ ] Roles, drawings, specifications, approvals, and change control
- [ ] Installation sequencing and dependencies
- [ ] Labelling, testing, commissioning, and acceptance
- [ ] As-built documentation, warranties, training, and spares
- [ ] Future expansion and lifecycle assumptions
